
# My Favorite Movie

## American Psycho (2000)

**Genre:** Psychological Thriller, Horror, Satire

### About the Movie
American Psycho follows Patrick Bateman, a wealthy investment banker in New York City who hides a disturbing and violent side behind his perfect appearance.

### Why I Like It
I like American Psycho because of its unique storytelling, psychological themes, dark humor, and Christian Bale's amazing performance as Patrick Bateman.

### Favorite Character
- Patrick Bateman (Christian Bale)

### Favorite Quote
> "I have to return some videotapes."

### My Rating
**10/10**
